## How the lineage emerged

The structure wasn't planned. The user fed pages from 5qln.com one at a time:
- The FCF book established the origin question
- The 5QLN Language book proved the grammar works
- The Codex gave the specification
- The agent activation gave the instruction set
- The five anchors gave the philosophy
- The design decisions gave the architecture
- The plugin and converter gave the implementation

Each entry landed and found its place in the growing structure. The agent's role: receive the URL, fetch, read, create the formation entry, place it. Not: catalog, enumerate, propose an order. The language writes the connections.

## Readiness for Phase 2

The skeleton (Phase 1) is substantially built. The wiki is ready for:
- **Phase 2: Reference index** — a single catalog page listing 300+ articles from 5qln.com with titles, dates, one-line summaries. The human can point at any entry and say "ingest that one."

## Structural principle

Entries are ordered by depth, not chronology. The first entry a reader should encounter is the origin question. The last is the execution. Between them: specification, instruction, philosophy, design, architecture, implementation. Same DNA, nine scales.
