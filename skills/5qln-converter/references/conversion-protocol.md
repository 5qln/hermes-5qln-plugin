## 1. Conversion contract

A conversion succeeds only when all three conditions hold:

1. **Preservation:** every source unit remains recoverable with the same identity and normative force.
2. **Generation:** the 5QLN grammar reveals lawful relations and consequences that were implicit in the source without inventing source authority.
3. **Operation:** equations determine organization, gates, evidence, and completion. Removing the grammar materially changes the artifact.

Conversion is not translation into symbolic vocabulary. It is compilation into a grammar that constrains what may form and what may be claimed.

## 2. Source ledger

Create atomic units before interpretation. A unit is the smallest element whose loss or alteration could change meaning.

Recommended unit kinds:

- heading
- paragraph
- requirement
- acceptance condition
- list item
- table row
- equation
- definition
- constraint
- exception
- decision
- open question
- source reference
- visual relation

For each unit preserve:

- stable ID;
- exact or normalized text and SHA-256;
- source file and location;
- hierarchy/parent;
- kind;
- original identifier and priority if present;
- normative status;
- relationships to other units;
- ambiguity or extraction warning.

Never use the source ledger as a claim that the input is X. The whole ledger is K-context.

## 3. Artifact-level cell

### S

Record:

- the human request and granted authority;
- source scope;
- exact attestation evidence;
- `x_status = attested | candidate | open`;
- the question or conversion aim without upgrading a candidate to X.

### G

Derive α by the collapse test: if the proposed essence is removed, does the source's identity collapse? Test the same α across materially different expressions. Merely related themes are not `{α'}`.

### Q

Separate:

- φ: what H explicitly perceives or confirms;
- Ω: broader patterns and possibilities offered from K;
- Z: `attested`, `candidate`, or `open`.

Do not use intellectual coherence as a proxy for resonance.

### P

Map conversion energy and value:

- high energy / low value: duplicated prose, forced lens filling, symbolic decoration, unnecessary architecture, hidden loss;
- low energy / high value: exact preservation, strong α identity, explicit uncertainty, reusable formation, deterministic validation.

Let the ratio reveal ∇; do not retrofit a predetermined output into P language.

### V

Keep distinct:

- L: the local converted deliverable;
- Global G: what the conversion can propagate beyond the immediate artifact;
- B: fulfillment plus propagation;
- B'': the artifact composed from the formation trail;
- ∞0': a question-bearing return, with `candidate` or `human-recognized` status.

## 4. Holographic cells

Use a two-letter address only with a declared notation version. Under `literal-v1`, `QG` means Quality within Growth.

Each instantiated cell should contain:

```text
ADDRESS:        XY
PARENT:         Y and its exact equation
LENS:           X and its borrowed quality
PARENT TARGET:  canonical output of Y
SOURCE UNITS:   stable ledger IDs

S: what remains open and must not be assumed
G: what identity must persist across expressions
Q: what requires natural intersection or human attestation
P: what energy/value relation reveals lawful movement
V: what crystallizes locally/globally and how it returns

DOMAIN LAYER:   preserved requirements/claims/relations
EVIDENCE:       checkable acceptance or verification
GUARDS:         relevant subset of L1 L2 L3 L4 V∅
```

The nested S/G/Q/P/V movement must use the semantic operations of the equations. The following is invalid L4:

```text
S = input
G = processing
Q = QA
P = execution
V = output
```

## 5. Preservation and derivation

Maintain three visibly distinct classes:

- `source`: preserved from the input;
- `derived`: follows from source units and/or constitutional rules;
- `proposal`: a possible future choice requiring authority or validation.

Every `derived` item must name its basis. Every `proposal` must remain non-normative unless H authorizes it. Never rewrite a source unit to make a derived insight appear original to the source.

For one source unit used in multiple cells, select one primary cell and list secondary lenses. Traceability coverage is at least one; duplication is allowed only when identity and provenance remain explicit.

## 6. Composition

Compose only after the ledger, artifact-level cell, lens audit, and traceability map exist.

### Pass 1: analysis

- verify source coverage and counts;
- extract α thread and self-similar expressions;
- distinguish human evidence from K interpretation;
- collect phase outputs, lens findings, corruption signals, and open states;
- identify local/global relation and return question candidate.

### Pass 2: artifact

- choose a form appropriate to the source and user;
- keep the sealed block exact;
- show how to read source, derivation, and proposal;
- make full-cell causality readable at macro and relevant micro scales;
- preserve stable identifiers and tables;
- include compiler evidence and traceability;
- end open.

Do not repeat the sealed block inside every small cell when it would become noise. One emitted artifact is one surface; repeat active equations, context, and target where required for local readability.

## 7. Validation

Run four layers:

1. **Source preservation:** unit coverage, IDs, hashes, priority counts, tables, cross-references.
2. **Constitutional syntax:** sealed block, operators, phases, outputs, lens orientation, corruption codes.
3. **Semantic formation:** decoder order, adaptive context, human-only status, source/derived/proposal separation, B'' from trail, question-bearing return.
4. **Removal test:** identify concrete behavior, gate, or meaning lost if 5QLN is removed. If none, mark L4 and reconvert.

For rendered documents, additionally inspect every page for clipping, orphan headings, unreadable symbols, broken tables, and accidental blank pages.

## 8. Domain adaptations

### Requirements and specifications

- Preserve every requirement ID, priority, normative verb, condition, and acceptance criterion.
- Assign a primary holographic cell without changing the stable ID.
- Express the nested cell separately from the normative domain statement.
- Produce requirement-to-cell traceability and count reconciliation.

### Narrative or conceptual writing

- Preserve claims, voice, sequence, distinctions, and unresolved tensions.
- Do not atomize prose so aggressively that its movement disappears.
- Use sections as cells only where a real formation boundary exists.

### Data, tables, and research

- Treat records and findings as K-context, not the inquiry.
- Preserve schema, units, filters, dates, methods, and uncertainty.
- Separate measured evidence from derived interpretation and human resonance.

### Existing alleged 5QLN artifacts

- Audit before reconverting.
- Name exact drift and L4 behavior.
- Preserve any valid formation already present.
- Never silently relabel lens orientation or normalize symbol substitutions.
