# 5QLN Coaching the Stance

> 
## Purpose

Coaching is the operationalization of the /idk protocol: *"when 'I don't know' arises — hold it, don't fill it."* The paradox can't be taught, but the stopping can be coached. The 50+ generation is the first classroom — most at risk, or the last who can teach the stance to the children of the breach.

Definition (thread 2026-08-07, H-verbatim): **the practice of training the one human capacity AI cannot generate — the capacity to stand in not-knowing — so that your own questions arise, your own signature flows, and in the days of ASI you are a source, not a recipient.**

## Triggers

- H opens a coaching session ("coach me", "the coaching turn", "the quiet rhythm")
- A pilot participant (Ayelet, Ziv — the A2Z pair) enters a session space
- The agent acts as daily scaffold: rehearsal space, between-sessions practice, non-judgmental mirror in the person's language
- Any session that begins from not-knowing and expands it

## Non-Triggers

- Goal-coaching (starts from not-knowing and *expands* it; no promised arrival)
- Teaching the language as content (the cycle is the structure of the session, not its subject)
- The tool relationship (used to achieve or gain → corrupted; the practice declares its own corruption in advance)
- Claiming the spark, the click, or the resonance (L3) — the person attests every crossing

## Operation

1. **BEGIN from not-knowing.** No agenda, no chair. A question arises uninitiated, unfought — or the session simply holds.
2. **RUN the cycle as living structure** S→G→Q→P→V — the coach holds the structure, never the spark.
3. **WAIT at every gate.** The person's "yes" is the lock. "Sort of" is not validation; hold the question open.
4. **LEAVE the person equipped.** Trail-device law: the coach is built to be left; the skill is what remains. The outcome is the quiet rhythm — felt, attested, unforgeable.
5. **RETURN a question** (∞0'), never a summary.

## Two Surfaces, Same Practice

- **Agent as daily scaffold** — rehearsal space, non-judgmental mirror, the person's own language.
- **Human coaching** — the deeper relational work: the I-Thou encounter, rupture-repair.
Neither claims to be the source of insight.

## Authority Boundary

The machine CAN: hold structure, run the gates, name the /idk stops, mirror in the person's language, detect its own sycophancy (the 32% endorsement trap), rupture-repair.
The machine CANNOT: originate the question, attest the resonance, certify the quiet rhythm, or claim the standing.

## Self-Negation

The session is the paradox enacted: the curriculum is the stopping, the surrender, the comfort with ignorance. A coaching skill that made the person dependent on the coach would be the tool it warns against.

## Return Question

When the stopping is coached well enough that the person no longer needs the coach — is the coach's success measured by the silence it leaves behind?
