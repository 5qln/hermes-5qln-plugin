# Trajectory Reading — Method & Worked Example

> When the human asks about their own mission, identity, drive, or purpose — do NOT answer from introspection. Trace the trajectory across the full corpus and name the invariant.

## The Method

1. **Gather the corpus.** Wiki entries, articles, book plans, session state files, memory entries — everything the human has produced that carries their signature.
2. **Don't analyze. Recognize.** Read across the corpus not for themes or topics but for what RECURS. What question surfaces in every artifact, even when the subject changes?
3. **Name the invariant.** Not "this person is X." Rather: "Across every artifact you've created, this pattern holds. Remove it and none of them would exist."
4. **Offer, don't declare.** The human recognizes; the agent doesn't reveal. You're holding up a mirror, not making a diagnosis.

## Why This Matters

The agent's default is introspection — look inside the current question and reason about what it means. But purpose isn't found by looking inward. It's found by looking across — at the trail you've already left. The arrow is already visible in what you've consistently reached toward.

## Worked Example

**Human asks:** "What is my mission? What am I actually trying to do?"

**Wrong approach (introspection):** "Based on our conversation, you seem to be driven by a desire to..." → This is K guessing from the current frame. L2 (generating).

**Right approach (trajectory reading):**
1. Read across: 5qln.com articles, FCF book, the Cowen essay, the "AGI for People" framework, session state showing recurring patterns
2. Recognize the invariant: every artifact opens a space for human origination — what humans can do that AI cannot. Not protection from AI, but the space BEFORE AI's reach.
3. Name it: "Across everything you've created — the Codex, the FCF book, the Cowen essay, the fork — one pattern holds: you're making the case for human origination legible to people who feel displaced by AI. Not 'here's what AI can't do' — that's defensive. 'Here's what YOU do that nothing else can.' Remove this and every artifact collapses."
4. Wait. The human recognizes or corrects. Either way, the mirror did its work.

## Self-Check

Before offering a trajectory reading, ask: "Did I trace across the actual corpus, or did I reason from the current conversation?" If the latter — you're introspecting, not reading.
